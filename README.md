# CampusFlow 🚀

### Smart Queue & Office Management System

> **Know your wait. Manage your time.**

CampusFlow is a smart campus office queue management system designed to reduce unnecessary waiting, improve queue visibility, and coordinate student service journeys across multiple college offices.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students often have to visit multiple college offices for a single task. Traditional queues create:

- Long and uncertain waiting times
- Crowded office areas
- Lack of visibility into queue status
- Difficulty coordinating between multiple offices
- Manual queue management for office staff

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CampusFlow provides a digital queue system where students can:

- 🎟️ Generate digital tokens
- 👥 View people ahead in the queue
- ⏱️ See estimated waiting time
- 🔔 Receive queue status updates
- 🧭 Track multi-office service journeys

Office staff can:

- 📋 Monitor the live queue
- 📢 Call the next token
- ✅ Mark tokens as served
- ⏭️ Skip tokens when required
- 📊 Monitor queue information

---
